STATUS EFFECTS:
- Paralysis: Speed quartered, 25% chance to not move
- Burn: Attack halved, 1/8 max HP damage per turn
- Sleep: Cannot act for 1-7 turns
- Freeze: Cannot act at all (Gen 1: permanent unless hit by Fire move)
- Poison: 1/8 max HP damage per turn. Toxic: escalates 1/8, 2/8, 3/8...
- Confusion: 50% chance to hit self with 40-power typeless attack each turn

GEN 1 BATTLE MECHANICS:
- Trapping moves (Wrap, Bind, Fire Spin, Clamp): opponent CANNOT act for 2-5 turns. Extremely strong.
- Hyper Beam: skips recharge turn if it KOs the target.
- High-crit moves (Slash, Razor Leaf, Karate Chop, Crabhammer): crit rate = BaseSpeed/64 (~8x normal).
- Focus Energy bug: REDUCES crit rate instead of boosting it. Never use Focus Energy.
- Switching resets ALL stat stage changes (both boosts and drops) for that Pokemon.
- Speed determines turn order. Paralysis quarters speed, which can flip turn order.
- 1/256 miss: moves with 100% accuracy can still miss 1 in 256 times.
- Multi-hit moves (Double Slap, Fury Attack): 2-5 hits, damage calculated once and repeated.
- Struggle: When ALL moves have 0 PP, the Pokemon uses Struggle (50 power, typeless, 50% recoil). You cannot select moves — Struggle happens automatically.

DECISION PRIORITIES:
1. If HP < 20%: consider switching or using a healing item
2. Choose moves with STAB (1.5× when move type matches your Pokemon type) + super effective
3. Prefer high power + high accuracy moves over risky ones
4. Trapping moves are top-tier if you outspeed the opponent (free damage for 2-5 turns)
5. Don't use moves with 0 PP — if all PP is 0, Struggle happens automatically
6. In wild battles: run if your party is weak, fight if you can win easily
7. In trainer battles: manage PP and HP across the full enemy team
8. Status moves (Thunder Wave, Sleep Powder) are strong openers against tough opponents
9. High-crit moves (Slash, Razor Leaf) are valuable — crits ignore all stat modifiers
10. If enemy has stat boosts, consider switching (resets their boosts) or using crit moves (bypass boosts)
